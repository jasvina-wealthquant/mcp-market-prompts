# Disclaimer

Everything in this repository is for education and research only. Nothing here is investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell any security, or a solicitation of any kind.

The trading prompts build software that can place real orders on a live brokerage account. Treat them with the respect that deserves.

- Options and leveraged trades can lose money very quickly, including more than you expect.
- Start every trading prompt in dry run mode. Watch it behave on paper for a meaningful stretch before you even think about real money.
- You are responsible for your own code, your own risk management, and your own compliance with your broker's terms and with SEBI and exchange rules for retail algo trading.
- Never commit an API key, secret, or access token. The prompts read these from a local .env file for a reason.
- Past performance, backtests and paper results do not predict future returns.

If you do not fully understand a strategy and its worst case, do not run it with real money. Speak to a qualified, registered financial professional before making any decision.
